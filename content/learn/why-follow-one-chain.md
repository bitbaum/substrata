A sector page is a filing cabinet. A chain is a story you can check.

Pick one constraint — a scanner, a steel, a licence. Then ask, in order:

1. **What is it**, in a sentence a non-specialist can hold.
2. **Who makes it**, and which of those rows is sourced.
3. **Which rules** speed it up or slow it down, and who asked for them in public.
4. **What science** would remove it, and how far off that is.
5. **Whether money** is actually the thing that is scarce.

Those are the Markets, Policy, Science and Capital pages. They are not alternative homepages. They are the next rooms once you have a bottleneck in hand.

If a room is empty, that is a research gap, not a missing menu. Use **Know something, or want us to look?** on the page. The inbox is private. Nothing publishes until a reviewer reads a source.
