## A map earns trust one link at a time

A research map is useful when you can follow a statement back to the record that supports it. A company name connected to a material can mean several things: a confirmed producer, a possible producer found in a search, or an analyst's lead. Those are different claims. Drawing them with the same confident line would make the picture easier to admire and harder to trust.

Substrata's [chain atlas](/atlas) groups the existing bottleneck records by research stage. Its bars count records in the corpus. They do not measure world production, market share, or the economic importance of a stage. An empty stage means the research has a gap.

## One corpus, several ways to read it

The atlas, [search](/search), company pages and assistant draw on the research records already used by the site. That means a corrected producer source can improve several surfaces at once. It also means an uncertainty label needs to survive every transformation.

The [data-quality page](/data) explains the evidence vocabulary and offers JSON and CSV exports. The JSON includes a content digest so two people can establish whether they analysed the same snapshot. Its generation timestamp is separate from the date an analyst last assessed a claim.

## Scores are arguments

A binding score combines four analyst judgements. It helps order a reading list; it does not turn uncertain research into a precise measurement. The rationale and assessment date belong beside the number. Readers should be able to disagree with the reasoning and submit a better source.

## Expertise is part of the system

People who qualify a material, ramp a process or connect a grid know things that a public document may omit. The [talent section](/talent) connects the project's existing expertise needs to a contribution path. These are requests for research help, not employment offers.

In [Substrata chat](/chat), asking the assistant and sending a contribution are separate actions. A contribution receives a receipt after it reaches the research inbox. It still needs review before it can change a sourced claim.

## Keep the development record close

The [roadmap](/roadmap), [changelog](/changelog) and [vision](/development) read the central development profile. They should describe the same project that readers use and builders work on. This article explains the design; the changelog records completed changes and the roadmap records unfinished work.
