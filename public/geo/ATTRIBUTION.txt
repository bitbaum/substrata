Map data sources and licenses
============================

Geometry and map viewpoint
--------------------------
The countries-50m.json geometry comes from Natural Earth 1:50m Admin-0
countries data, distributed in world-atlas 2.0.2. Natural Earth data is in the
public domain. Its default country layer depicts de facto control; this is one
map viewpoint, not a legal conclusion.
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/50m-cultural-vectors/50m-admin-0-countries-2/
https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Country identifiers and derived data
-------------------------------------
The ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 lookup used for map feature identifiers comes from
world-countries 5.1.0 by Mohammed Le Doze:
https://github.com/mledoze/countries

Contains information from world-countries, which is made available under the
Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/

The derived countries-50m.json and countries-index.json database resources are
available under ODbL-1.0. The generation method is scripts/geo/build-world-50m.mjs.
